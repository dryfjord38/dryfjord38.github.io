PRONUNCIATION

In this section, you will find all the lessons about the pronunciation of french. Videos explaining how to make certain sounds will be accessible on the External Resources Page.

IMPORTANT : These lessons explain the global tendencies of pronunciation, and not the absolute truth of real french pronunciation : it evolves, vary depending on the region, or simply doesn't follow the rules sometimes. The best way to pronounce french the right way is to practice it regularly, ask native speakers and actively try to recognize patterns : at some point you'll catch yourself guessing the pronunciation right !
