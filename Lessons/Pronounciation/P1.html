PRONUNCIATION : ALPHABET AND SOUNDS
The Alphabet

The french alphabet has 26 letters : a b c d e f g h i j k l m n o p q r s t u v w x y z

Some of these letters can have accents : à â ç é è ê ë î ï ù

The Sounds

French has a total of 37 sounds (18 consonants, 12 Oral vowels, 4 Nasal vowels and 3 Semi-consonants).

Note : The sounds /ɑ/ and /œ̃/ are now pronounced as respectively /a/ and /ɛ̃/, but I think it was interested to put them anyways in the list of french sounds.

The following tables contain all the sounds, ordered by categories (consonants, vowels, nasal vowels and semi-consonants).

Under these tables, you fill find a more detailed explanation of each sound, with additionnal examples associated to images.
